frequently asked

Where is my birthday stored?

Only on your iPhone, in the app's own local storage. It is never uploaded, synced, backed up to a server, or shared with anyone — including me.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Touch and hold the app icon on your Home Screen, choose Remove App, then Delete App. Your saved birthday is removed along with it. There's nothing stored anywhere else to clean up.

Does it work offline?

Yes, entirely. The app makes no network requests at all, so it works the same in airplane mode, on a plane, or with no signal.

Can I use it on iPad or Mac?

It's built for iPhone in portrait orientation. It will run on an iPad in iPhone compatibility mode, but the layout is designed for a phone screen.

privacy

This app collects no data whatsoever.

  • —No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs.
  • —No account, no sign-in, no email address required.
  • —No network requests of any kind. Nothing leaves your device because nothing is ever sent.
  • —Your birthday is saved in local app storage on your iPhone and used only to calculate the numbers you see on screen.
  • —Deleting the app deletes that birthday. No copy of it exists anywhere else.
  • —Nothing is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because there is nothing to sell or share.

Read the full privacy policy. If it ever changes, it will change there first, along with its effective date.
